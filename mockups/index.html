tradebotredesign mockups

Research to live, one calm instrument panel

Static mockups of every screen in the PyQt6 app, redrawn as one system called Graphite. They are plain HTML, so open any page and click around: the theme, the dry-run / sandbox / live chip, and the kill switch in the status bar all work.

Mode is never ambiguousA permanent status bar shows Dry run, Sandbox or Live and holds the kill switch. Live also draws a red line across the top of the window.
Colour never carries meaning aloneUp is blue-teal, down is vermilion, and every figure also gets a drawn triangle. It works for red-green colour blindness and for greyscale screenshots.
Settings stay beside their resultsBacktest, Evolve and Screener all use the same left setup pane and right results pane, so a strategy moves between them without relearning the layout.
Dense, quiet, one accentOne typeface with tabular figures, warm graphite neutrals, and a single violet accent used for selection and the primary action only.

What changes from today

NavigationEmoji nav buttons become a grouped rail (Markets, Strategies, Account) with drawn icons and shortcuts. Kill switch, About and Settings no longer sit in the nav list.
Glass themeTranslucent panels over a blurred gradient are replaced by solid panels. They stay legible on any OS and keep every chart's gridlines readable. The old Glass theme stays selectable.
SettingsA modal with an icon list becomes a full page with one section per topic, saved as you change it.
Backtest reportEquity, drawdown, trade statistics, Monte Carlo and the monthly heatmap are one scrolling report with the same stat strip on top.
EvolveThe effort presets (Fast, Good, Best, Auto) move to the top bar as one control; the winning strategy is shown as readable text next to the score curve.
DialogsOne dialog shape with a footer for actions. Anything that isn't a decision (settings, results, scripts) is a page or a dock, not a modal.
